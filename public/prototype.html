03 — Architecture

Four specialist agents.
One coherent document.

Each agent has a single specialty and hands off to the next. Output stays consistent across a 50-page document because the orchestration is deterministic, not chat-based.

Agent 01 · Ingestion

Discovery agent

Reads the meeting — whether captured by Fireflies, Read.ai, Otter, or your own typed notes. Extracts the fact find, stated goals, the implicit concerns, household composition, and any specific instructions the adviser gave mid-call. Structures it as a profile ready for downstream agents.

ingest time
Fireflies · Read.ai · Otter · plain text · PDF upload · voice memo
Agent 02

Profile agent

Cross-references the meeting against your firm's prior reports. Identifies which sections of your template need the most attention. Flags ambiguity for adviser confirmation before drafting.

structured profile · section priorities · clarification flags
Agent 03

Template agent

Populates your firm's template — the same one you uploaded. Writes in your voice and structure. Recommendations come from your firm's approved universe, not external lookups.

Word · Keynote · PDF · PowerPoint
Agent 04 · Review

Verification agent

Final pass for numerical consistency across the document, typo detection, disclosure language compliance with the regulator in the client's jurisdiction, and adherence to the firm-specific style your prior reports established.

SCA · FCA · DFSA · FSRA · firm style adherence

05 — Why Advisens

“Why can't I just use ChatGPT?”

A reasonable question. The honest answer is that an LLM and a multi-agent report platform built for advisory work are not in the same category.

The anchor argument

Your report is your infrastructure. It is what sells you.

Two advisers, same prospect, same discovery meeting. By coincidence both arrive at the same recommendation. The prospect now decides on two things: who did they get on with, and whose work landed better when it hit the table. The adviser running ChatGPT cut and paste will not touch the sides of the adviser running four specialist agents that improve every single time they ship.

Option A

Generic LLM, manual stitching

A single chat window with no memory of your firm. You type "rewrite this section with these new client details," paste, format, fact check, hope the disclosure language is right. Next prospect, you repeat the entire exercise from scratch.

  • ·The report stays at the same quality level forever. No compounding improvement, just copy and paste.
  • ·Every output is a one off. The voice drifts, structure varies, language slips between reports.
  • ·No firm context. You can paste your template, but the LLM has no memory of the 100+ other reports you have shipped.
  • ·Disclosures are at the operator's discretion. A general model will skip, paraphrase, or fabricate regulatory wording.
  • ·Your clients never see you improving. The materials look the same as they did a year ago.
Option B

Advisens, four agents working together

A specialist orchestration designed only for advisory work. Discovery, Profile, Template and Verification agents hand off automatically, each one carrying your firm's voice and product universe. Every report you ship makes the next one better.

  • Quality compounds with use. The agents learn your structure, recommendation patterns, and house style every time you ship.
  • Consistency across the whole library. Voice, structure, numeric cross references, disclosure language, all locked.
  • Fact checked by a dedicated agent. Numbers reconciled across sections, typos flagged, regulator specific wording verified.
  • Your IP, in your firm's product universe. Recommendations only ever come from products your firm has signed off on.
  • Adviser time, recovered. Paraplanners and advisers spend less on report mechanics, more on client work.
